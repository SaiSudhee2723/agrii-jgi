Agricultural Intelligence

Precision farming tools powered by computer vision & machine learning

From soil optical quality assessments to weather-adjusted irrigation scheduling and live Mandi market intelligence, Geosense delivers software-first agronomic decision support.

🧪

AI Soil Optical Diagnostics

Capture soil via mobile camera. Our OpenCV pipeline evaluates blur, glare, lighting balance, and visible soil texture/hue with rigorous scientific transparency.

🌾

ML Crop Recommendation

Trained Random Forest model correlates soil parameters (N, P, K, pH) with temperature, humidity, and rainfall to predict optimal crop yields and management packages.

💧

Smart Irrigation Engine

Calculates crop evapotranspiration (FAO-56) across growth stages and subtracts local forecast precipitation to provide exact liter demand per acre.

⛅

Agro-Weather Telemetry

Live Open-Meteo forecasts combined with automated farming hazard alerts for heat stress, foliar pest risks, and heavy rainfall drainage advisories.

APMC Mandi Intelligence

Track modal, minimum, and maximum commodity prices across regional agricultural markets with 7-day trend trajectories and transparent benchmark labeling.

Farmer Community

Scientific Methodology

Data integrity without fabricated sensor claims

We uphold strict scientific ethics: visible consumer RGB mobile cameras capture surface reflectance and cannot measure chemical NPK concentrations without laboratory chemical assays or calibrated spectroscopy. Geosense provides observable physical diagnostics and clearly directs users to accredited laboratories for soil chemistry.
